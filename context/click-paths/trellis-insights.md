# Click path: Trellis insights → action

**Status:** desired click path, from the SE (2026-10-08). Not yet walked in
the demo tenant, so confirm Trellis is enabled there before building a demo
on it.

The idea: start with a question, get a summary, drill into the data behind
it, then act on it in another part of Sprout.

## 1. Start with a question
Open the **Trellis** chat. It's reachable from the main dashboard and from
inside modules like **Listening** and **Reporting**. Type a question or pick a
pre-built prompt.

Example prompts:
- "What themes are trending in my listening dashboard?"
- "Compare post performance from this week to last week."
- "What's driving the most engagement?"

## 2. Get the summary
Trellis returns a high-level summary: key themes, strategic insights, and
suggested next steps, often with charts or tables.

## 3. Drill into the data
Click straight into the data points Trellis surfaced:
- **Sentiment spike on a certain day:** click that day in the chart to see the exact messages behind it.
- **Top-performing theme:** click the theme to see every conversation and post related to it.

## 4. Act in the relevant module
Pick the branch that fits the insight:
- **Listening → Publishing:** a trending topic or strong content theme → open the **Publishing calendar** and draft and schedule content that builds on it.
- **Insights → Reporting:** a strong insight about competitive performance → open **Reporting** and build a custom report that shows it to stakeholders.
- **Inbox analysis → strategy:** key themes from customer messages in the **Smart Inbox** → feed them into community management strategy or product feedback.

## Notes for demos built on this path
- Trellis answers come from the tenant's real data (D6), so they'll describe Secure Patient Technology or Snouts, Paws & Tails, not the prospect. The overlay renames brand and profile names in the answer text, but the themes and numbers stay the tenant's. Prepare the prompts in advance and check the answers before the call
- Never present a Trellis answer about tenant data as the prospect's own results (CLAUDE.md rule 4)
