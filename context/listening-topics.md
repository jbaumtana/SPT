# Listening topic templates

Placeholders in `{braces}` come from the prospect brief.

| Template | Query shape | Verticals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brand health | `{brand}` OR `{handles}` OR `{product_names}` | All |
| Competitive share of voice | `{brand}` vs `{competitors}` | All |
| Crisis / issue watch | `{brand}` AND (outage OR recall OR closure OR breach) | All |
| Patient experience | `{brand}` AND (wait time OR ER OR appointment OR billing) | Healthcare |
| Constituent sentiment | `{agency}` AND (`{programs}` OR services OR office) | Public Sector |
| Guest experience | `{brand}` AND (check-in OR room OR staff OR booking OR refund) | Travel & Hospitality |
