# Playbook: Travel & Hospitality
Last updated: 2026-10-07 · Demos run: 0 · Won: 0 · **Status: starting hypotheses, validate on real deals**

## Buyer map
Brand/marketing director (owner), social team, guest experience/care lead,
property or regional managers (multi-location contributors).

## Vocabulary
Guests, properties/locations, stays, bookings, loyalty members, reviews,
peak season, occupancy.

## Story arc
1. Many properties, one brand → **multi-location publishing, brand consistency**
2. Guests complain publicly and expect fast answers → **Smart Inbox + reviews**
3. Reputation drives bookings → **review management, listening**
4. Prove what works → **reporting by property/region, competitor benchmarks**

## Data patterns
- Profiles: brand account + per-property accounts + Google Business per location
- Content: destination inspiration, seasonal offers, loyalty, UGC reposts (invented creators), events
- Inbox: booking issues, refund requests, praise for (invented) staff, reviews 1–5 stars, a disruption spike (weather, outage)
- Sentiment: mostly positive, with one clear negative cluster to resolve live
