## Buyer map
Communications director / PIO (owner), digital or social lead, agency or
department contributors, records/legal, IT/procurement.

## Vocabulary
Constituents/residents, departments, PIO, public records, emergency
communications, accessibility, procurement, FedRAMP/StateRAMP (verify what applies).

## Story arc
1. Dozens of departments posting on their own → **central governance, approvals, permissions**
2. Emergencies need fast, consistent messaging → **scheduling, cross-department publishing**
3. Residents ask questions everywhere → **Smart Inbox, response tracking**
4. Accountability → **reporting, records retention story**
