# Playbook: Healthcare
Last updated: 2026-10-07 · Demos run: 0 · Won: 0 · **Status: starting hypotheses, validate on real deals**

## Buyer map
Marketing/comms director (owner), social manager (daily user), patient
experience or care lead (inbox), compliance/legal (veto power).

## Vocabulary
Patients (not customers), service lines, locations/facilities, providers,
patient experience, HIPAA, PHI, open enrollment, flu season.

## Story arc
1. Many locations and service lines, inconsistent voice → **publishing + approvals**
2. Patients DM about appointments and bills → **Smart Inbox routing, take it offline**
3. Leadership wants proof → **reporting by service line**
4. Reputation risk → **listening for patient-experience spikes**

## Data patterns
- Content pillars: patient education, community health, provider spotlights, careers/recruiting, events
- Seasonal: flu shots, open enrollment, awareness months
- Inbox: appointment questions, billing complaints, praise for named (invented) nurses, wrong-channel PHI that must be redirected
- Profiles: parent system + facility or service-line accounts, Google Business per location

## Landmines
- No PHI in generated posts or replies. Inbox replies must route sensitive issues offline
- No outcome or efficacy claims in post copy
- Approval workflow must visibly include compliance review
