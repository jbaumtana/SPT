A caption on the calendar is several elements, so a selector that matches the pieces replaces only part of it.

**What happened.** The caption selector matched 291 pieces across 66 posts. Hashtags and links are separate pieces, so the tenant's own hashtags and links stayed visible beside the new text.

**Why it mattered.** Half-replaced captions look worse than untouched ones.

**What to do.** Select the whole caption and record what is inside it. Mark each selector in the app map as replacing a whole caption or one piece.
