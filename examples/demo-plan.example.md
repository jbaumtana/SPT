# Demo plan: Bayline Health (example)

**Playbook:** `context/playbooks/healthcare.md` · **Length:** 30 min · **Date:** 2026-10-14

## Story
Bayline's facilities post independently, and compliance only sees posts after
they're live. Patients' billing DMs sit unanswered for days. We open on
governed publishing (the comms director's top pain and the compliance
counsel's reason for being there), move to inbox routing for billing DMs, and
close on a recruiting-impact report the VP can take to the board.

## Screens
| # | Screen | Answers pain | Min |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Publishing calendar (system + 3 facilities) | Facilities posting on their own | 5 |
| 2 | Composer → approval with compliance step | Legal finds out after the fact | 7 |
| 3 | Smart Inbox, billing DM routed and taken offline | Missed billing DMs | 8 |
| 4 | Report: careers content → applicant clicks | Proving impact on recruiting | 6 |
